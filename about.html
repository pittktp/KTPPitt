President's Welcome

Welcome to the Alpha Chapter of Kappa Theta Pi, Michigan's premier professional technology fraternity. On behalf of our chapter, I am excited to welcome you to our fraternity's website, where you can catch a glimpse of the passion and excellence that our chapter celebrates.

Kappa Theta Pi offers brothers the support to be extraordinary during their time at Michigan with resources centered around five pillars: professional development, alumni connections, social growth, technological advancement, and academic support. From project teams and study groups to professional development workshops and hackathons / design jams, we foster a culture of growth encouraging members to pursue their tech passions. Our chapter values diversity, with brothers contributing unique experiences and excelling as student leaders. We celebrate our diverse brotherhood, welcoming all united by a passion for technology.

Reflecting on my time at Michigan, KTP has been the most impactful part of my college experience. I have witnessed the growth of some of Michigan's most brilliant and ambitious professionals, seeing first-hand the incredible things they do. More importantly, I have been introduced to lifelong friends and connections who serve as a constant source of inspiration and always support me to be my full and best self. I invite you to explore our website and learn more about our brotherhood.

With love,
Helen Liang
President, 2025

Our Pillars

We stand on five main pillars that guide our chapter and help members grow academically, socially, and professionally.

🎒

Professional Development

Through workshops, mentorship, and company recruiting, we prepare members for success in technology careers.

🌐

Alumni Connections

Our alumni network connects members with industry opportunities across tech and beyond.

👥

Social Growth

Exclusive social events and traditions help members build lasting friendships and community.

🎮

Technical Advancement

Project teams, hackathons, and workshops make it easy for members to expand their technical expertise.

🎓

Academic Support

Study groups and mentoring help members achieve academic excellence while balancing campus life.

History

Since our founding, Kappa Theta Pi at Pitt has grown into a thriving chapter focused on developing technical leaders and supporting one another through academics and professional growth.

From our early pledge classes to the current brothers, our history is defined by collaboration, resilience, and a commitment to service. We honor the traditions that shaped us while adapting to meet the needs of today's students.

DEI Commitment

We believe a strong chapter is one that reflects the diversity of the communities we serve. KTP is committed to creating an inclusive environment where all members feel seen, heard, and empowered.

Our DEI initiatives include outreach to underrepresented groups in tech, inclusive recruitment practices, and programming that centers diverse perspectives. We continually evaluate our practices to ensure equity in leadership, opportunities, and access.
