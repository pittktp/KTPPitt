From The President

Hello there! It is a pleasure to welcome you to Kappa Theta Pi’s website. KTP is Pitt’s premier co-ed professional technology fraternity, driven to provide academic, professional, and social growth for each of our more than 60 brothers.

For as much as popular culture loves the cliché of the anti-social computer nerd who can only communicate in ones and zeros, the modern tech industry needs more than someone who can only code. It needs interdisciplinary employees who are confident, multifaceted, and always ready to learn; the leaders, communicators, critical thinkers, and creatives who are as strong on a team as they are by themselves. In other words, the industry needs people. Not just robots. And KTP is focused on assisting our brothers in developing a wide range of skills, knowledge, and connections that will get them through the door—whatever that door may be for them—and continue to help them for the rest of their professional careers.

Through our diverse offering of events and opportunities, KTP facilitates an environment of self-improvement, exploration, and fun. We are more than just a student organization; we are a brotherhood—a group of unique tech-lovers who are always ready to support one another, reach out when someone needs a hand, and be there when the demands of the semester feel insurmountable. Whether you’re a senior looking for more friends in your upper-level electives or a freshman still deciding what to major in, there is a space for you in KTP. I can confidently say my time in college would not be as incredible as it has been without the wonderful and inspiring people that I now call brothers—and the only way this community would be more remarkable was if you were in it too.

Hail to Pitt, and Hail to KTP!

Best,

Natalie Goldsworthy
President of Kappa Theta Pi

Our Pillars

We stand on five main pillars that guide our chapter and help members grow academically, socially, and professionally.

🎒

Professional Development

Through workshops, mentorship, and company recruiting, we prepare members for success in technology careers.

🌐

Alumni Connections

Our alumni network connects members with industry opportunities across tech and beyond.

👥

Social Growth

Exclusive social events and traditions help members build lasting friendships and community.

🎮

Technical Advancement

Project teams, hackathons, and workshops make it easy for members to expand their technical expertise.

🎓

Academic Support

Study groups and mentoring help members achieve academic excellence while balancing campus life.

History

Since our founding, Kappa Theta Pi at Pitt has grown into a thriving chapter focused on developing technical leaders and supporting one another through academics and professional growth.

From our early pledge classes to the current brothers, our history is defined by collaboration, resilience, and a commitment to service. We honor the traditions that shaped us while adapting to meet the needs of today's students.

DEI Commitment

We believe a strong chapter is one that reflects the diversity of the communities we serve. KTP is committed to creating an inclusive environment where all members feel seen, heard, and empowered.

Our DEI initiatives include outreach to underrepresented groups in tech, inclusive recruitment practices, and programming that centers diverse perspectives. We continually evaluate our practices to ensure equity in leadership, opportunities, and access.
