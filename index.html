About Us

Kappa Theta Pi at the University of Pittsburgh is committed to bringing students together that have a shared passion for technology. We are the first professional technology fraternity in the country with Pitt as the beta chapter. Our members build a strong community focused on professional development, academic support, philanthropic initiatives, and social connections.
